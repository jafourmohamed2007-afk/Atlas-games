How to play

Move with WASD or the arrows (on phones, drag the left half of the screen). Your weapons attack by themselves. Pick up gems to level up and choose one of 3-4 upgrades (Reroll or Skip if you like). Max a weapon and own its paired passive, then open a boss chest to evolve it. Break lanterns for items. P or Esc pauses. Survive 15:00, then defeat the Pale Reaper. Spend gold in the Ember Shrine between runs.

About Ashen Vigil: Night of a Thousand

A dark-fantasy pixel survivor roguelite. Your weapons fire on their own: dodge swarms of bats, ghouls, skull archers and wraiths, collect soul gems, pick upgrades, evolve your weapons and survive 15 minutes until the Pale Reaper comes. With a narrated voice, boss chests and a permanent Ember Shrine shop. Free online, no download.

Questions
